# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Factory operators monitoring the production line, telemetry, and equipment state. The operator-workspace framing was confirmed during the October 2026 redesign.

## Product Purpose

Smart Factory combines a seven-stage manufacturing digital twin, plant KPIs, PLC parameters, and connected enterprise workspaces in a React dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the existing manufacturing stages, PLC data sources, RFID gate, motor and emergency controls, workspace launchers, and integration modals. Scene navigation and display settings must not send equipment commands. Some data is simulated; display source state honestly. Existing project uses React, TypeScript, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Zustand, and Vite.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing source, manufacturing layout, and user-provided dashboard screenshot are the reference for product content and behavior. No new performance, uptime, or production claims are authorized.

## Product Principles

- Keep the production model central and easy to inspect.
- Make measurements, states, and control consequences legible.
- Preserve working integrations and current local development changes.
